## Answer
Ahlqvist et al. (2018) justified K=5 by first running TwoStep clustering in the Swedish ANDIS cohort using silhouette width to choose cluster count, then assigning clusters with k-means. They did not rely on internal metrics alone: they replicated the five-cluster structure in external cohorts and showed clinically meaningful differences across groups (age at diagnosis, BMI, HbA1c, insulin resistance/secretion, and complication patterns). Reported justification therefore combined internal cluster-count selection with external reproducibility and clinical interpretability, rather than a single numeric silhouette threshold.
