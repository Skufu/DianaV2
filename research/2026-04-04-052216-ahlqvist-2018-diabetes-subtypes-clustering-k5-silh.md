## Answer
Ahlqvist et al. (2018) justified K=5 by first running TwoStep clustering in the Swedish ANDIS cohort using silhouette width to choose cluster count, then assigning clusters with k-means. Their justification was not silhouette-only: they also demonstrated replication of the five-cluster structure in independent cohorts and clinical separation across phenotypes and outcomes (age at diagnosis, BMI, HbA1c, insulin secretion/resistance, and complication risk). They did not foreground a single numeric silhouette cutoff as the main argument; instead they combined internal cluster-count selection with external reproducibility and clinical interpretability.
