## Answer
Adults 45-60 already show age-related usability decline and presbyopia effects; use larger legible text with comfortable line spacing, maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text (prefer higher in glare/low-vision conditions), avoid color-only meaning by adding labels/icons/patterns and clear focus states, and use at least 1 cm x 1 cm touch targets with sufficient spacing to reduce mistaps.
