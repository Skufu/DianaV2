Following Slieker et al. (2021), we adapted Ahlqvist clustering to features measurable in routine screening. While TG/HDL ratio is primarily an insulin resistance marker, in the context of low BMI and younger age, elevated TG/HDL reflects severe metabolic derangement characteristic of insulin-deficient states. The sequential assignment (LAP for SIRD first, then TG/HDL among remaining) mitigates confounding between SIRD and SIDD, as validated by Slieker's 85-97% cross-cohort SIDD sensitivity.
